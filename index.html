Curriculum vitae & research portfolio

Summary

Kaushalya is an immunologist and molecular biologist with a PhD in Medicine from Deakin University, a Master of Biotechnology and Bioinformatics, and a BSc (Hons) in Biomedical Science. Over five years of hands-on research spans immune cell biology, cytokine receptor characterisation, molecular assay development, gene editing, imaging, and bioinformatic data analysis, complemented by prior diagnostic laboratory experience within an ISO-certified quality management system in Sri Lanka.

Experience

2020 – 2025

Graduate Researcher / PhD Candidate

Deakin University

Independently designed, executed, and troubleshot experiments identifying and validating NK cell marker candidates in a zebrafish model, including selecting candidate markers from a published single-cell dataset and performing motif analysis (FIMO) on novel gene promoters. Designed a transgenic zebrafish model incorporating GFP into the genome to track NK cells, generated CRISPR-based mutants, and used gene cloning, competent cell preparation, and whole-mount in-situ hybridisation (WISH) alongside immunofluorescence microscopy to characterise immune cell populations. Developed and validated qPCR, dPCR, HRM, and Sanger sequencing assays, and supervised undergraduate and junior research students.
