Projects

Research projects from my PhD, Master's, and collaborative work.

Identifying Natural Killer cell markers in zebrafish

PhD research · Deakin University · 2020 – 2025

My doctoral research designed a transgenic zebrafish model incorporating GFP to track Natural Killer cells, alongside CRISPR-based mutants, gene cloning, and whole-mount in-situ hybridisation, to identify and validate candidate NK cell markers. This work also drew on a published single-cell dataset and motif analysis of gene promoters.

Characterisation of cytokine receptor CRLF3 in zebrafish

Master's research · Deakin University · 2021

Investigated the role and functional properties of CRLF3 within immune cell populations using a CRISPR/Cas9 knockout mutant, analysed via qPCR. Published in Frontiers in Immunology.
